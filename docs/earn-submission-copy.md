## Builder reflection (~200 words)
**Reflection: from "22 repos" to a single product pitch — my Road to Colosseum.**

Like most builders I kept optimizing the wrong metric: repo count. The Colosseum process
forced the shift every founder actually needs — from "look how much I can build" to
"this ONE thing is worth your money."

What changed:
1. **Portfolio pruning.** I stopped polishing 20 parallel bounty projects and concentrated
   the CWF energy into Z-Spend — an agentic treasury whose budget is enforced on-chain.
   The whole pitch became: "an AI agent that cannot overspend, even if its own key is
   stolen."
2. **Evidence beats effort.** Judges don't have time for "and then it works." Z-Spend's
   pitch is a 46-second video of real testnet transactions: $80 approved, $160 rejected
   by policy, then a $100 payment the chain itself reverted when the key fund was
   exhausted. One file replaced ten paragraphs.
3. **One builder, one product, one story.** The Colosseum one-product rule wasn't a
   constraint — it was the clarity I'd been avoiding.

Reflection chronicle built as an interactive log: https://sidsri14.github.io/colosseum-builders-reflect/. If you're 20-polishing-your
portfolio, stop: pick the product with the sharpest single sentence and shoot its proof.
